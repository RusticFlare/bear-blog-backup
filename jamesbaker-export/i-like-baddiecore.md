I was reading **Metal Hammer** magazine the other day, and they had an interview with one of my favourite bands: **Dayseeker**.

In the article they introduced me to the term _**<q>Baddiecore</q>**_:

> _<q>metalcore with enough pop music crossover and sex appeal that normie hot people like it</q>_\
> – Craig Reynolds (**Stray From The Path**) 

As well as Dayseeker, other Baddiecore bands are apparently **Sleep Token**, **Spiritbox**, **Bring Me The Horizon** and **HEALTH**.

I guess I’m into Baddiecore.
