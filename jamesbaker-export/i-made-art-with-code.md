At the beginning of this year I made sixty-four images with code. I didn’t quite manage one a day, but it was close. I used [OPENRNDR](tab:https://openrndr.org/) and all the code is on [my GitHub](tab:https://github.com/RusticFlare/openrndr-projects/tree/main/src/main/kotlin/uk/jamesbaker/openrndr/everyday).

I took a break at sixty-four because it’s $2^6$ – meaning my images are numbered `0` to `111111` in binary. Yes, I am a nerd!
