One consequence of taking photos on film, is that I’ll usually come back from a holiday with a partially finished roll. That’s exactly what happened when I came back from our trip to Italy in May. I finally got through that roll, and received the last of my scans at the end of June!

I’ve written before about [how I travel with my camera](https://jamesbaker.uk/tourism-and-photography/) – especially through street photography.
