We don’t have recurring subscriptions to streaming services like Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+, etc.

If there’s a show on a streaming service that we want to watch – like the final season of <cite>Stranger Things</cite> – we’ll pay for Netflix, then immediately cancel the subscription. This is effectively us buying a one-month pass. When that month expires, if there’s still more we want to watch – <cite>Black Mirror</cite>? – then we’ll get another month, and cancel it again immediately.

The benefits:

- We save money
- We spend less time watching TV
- We’re more deliberate about what we do watch

The downsides:

- …?

Try it! Go and cancel all your subscriptions. And only pay for them as and when you want to use them.
