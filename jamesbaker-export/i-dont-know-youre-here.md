This website is for me. I’ll probably do a future post about what that means. For now though, I want to share how I avoid seeing any the site’s metics.

> _<q>What gets measured, gets done.</q>_

I host this site on [Bear Blog](tab:https://bearblog.dev/), which measures a few thing by default:

- Page views  
- Post upvotes

Here’s how I turn them off.

## Don’t Collect Analytics

On your Dashboard, go to <q>Settings</q> and then <q>Advanced settings</q>. Uncheck <q>Collect analytics</q> and hit <q>Save</q>.

## Hide Upvote Counts

Add this to your theme’s CSS:
```
#upvote-form .upvote-button .upvote-count {  
  display: none;  
}  
```

Click <q>Save</q>. Boom! Gone!

## Hide Yourself From Your Discover

You can hide any blog from your [Discover](tab:https://bearblog.dev/discover/) feed. I hide my own. It’s not like _I_ need to discover it.
