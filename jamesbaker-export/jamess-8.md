As I sat down at my mum’s house – while visiting for an Easter meal – I spotted a couple of CDs she’d put out for us.

One was <cite>**James’s 8**</cite>: an eight song mixtape I made when I was _really_ young – I have no memory of it whatsoever. The other CD was the same thing, but by my younger brother.

<cite>**James’s 8**</cite> (circa 1999) track listing:

1. <cite>Dizzy!</cite> – Bob the Builder
2. <cite>American Pie</cite> – Don McLean
3. <cite>Streets of London</cite> – Rodger Whittaker
4. <cite>Mambo No. 5</cite> – Bob the Builder
5. <cite>Can We Fix It?</cite> – Bob the Builder
6. <cite>Aisy Waisy (Stimulater’s Radio Mix)</cite> – Cartoons
7. <cite>Rock DJ</cite> – Robbie Williams
8. <cite>It’s Raining Men</cite> – Geri Halliwell

All killer, no filler!
