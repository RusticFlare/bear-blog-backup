I just got the CD for an album I can no longer listen to on Spotify.

The deluxe edition of Miss May I’s <cite>[Monument](tab:https://album.link/s/63qEPI2UeYd4B3sKUzWIfB)</cite> has four extra songs that weren’t part of the original release. I’d previously saved the album to my Spotify library and some of the extra tracks are on my playlists. Now they're greyed out and I can't listen to them – probably because of a licensing issue (I’m in the UK).

If you use a subscription music service like Spotify, they can remove any tracks at any point. The only way to protect yourself is to own the music – either physically, or with DRM-free files.

I’m still using Spotify for now, but I dream of a day when I’ll be free of it.
