I recently finished reading the first arc of <cite>[The Stormlight Archive](tab:https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/the-stormlight-archive-series)</cite> by Brandon Sanderson. My brother got me all five 1,000+ page books and both tie-in novellas for my 30^th^ birthday. I loved all of them!

For me, what differentiated it from other epic fantasies was the focus on mental health. The genre hallmarks are all there: magic, politics, battles, loads of characters. I just wasn’t expecting the most impactful moments (for me at least) being when the characters overcome the struggles with their own minds.

I’ve seen a few posts by other bloggers about fiction being better than self-help[^raccoon][^absurd-pirate][^suliman], and it’s down to books like <cite>Stormlight</cite> that mean I wholeheartedly agree. <cite>Stormlight</cite> definitely hit home more than <cite>Atomic Habits</cite> or <cite>The Little Book of Ikigai</cite> did. Plus, <cite>Stormlight</cite> was a way better read.

I can’t sing the praises of this series enough. If it sounds like it’s up your street, take this as the sign to start the first book: <cite>The Way of Kings</cite>. And – because the first arc is finished – you won’t be left in limbo waiting for the next in the series (I’m looking at you: George R. R. Martin and Patrick Rothfuss).
