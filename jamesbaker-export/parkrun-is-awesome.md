I’ve stated doing parkrun a bit more regularly. I did my first one in Harrogate at the start of 2019, and by the start of August 2025 (6 years later) I’d done 8. I’ve now completed 14 in the last 10 months, taking my total to 22.

For those that don’t know, here’s what parkrun is (from [their website](tab:https://www.parkrun.org.uk/)):

> parkrun is a free, community event where you can walk, jog, run, volunteer or spectate. parkrun is 5k and takes place every Saturday morning.
> 
> parkrun is positive, welcoming and inclusive, there is no time limit and no one finishes last. Everyone is welcome to come along.

A quick note on the volunteers: my local parkrun (York) gets about 700 runners a week (on Christmas day it was 1170), so it wouldn’t be possible without the volunteers that marshal and time-keep every event. A massive thank you to them!

My old Saturday morning run routine was a more casual jog along the riverbank for an hour or so. Whereas now I’m doing that run on a Friday morning, and parkrun on a Saturday. 

Part of that it the social side. A lot of my friends and family do parkrun, sometimes we do them together, but you can always compare times and see how each other’s doing.   

Last weekend I was on a stag-do in Newcastle, and I still managed to get up and do the Leazes parkrun (the stag and best-man said they’d come, but their hangovers had other plans). I was also hungover (like I said: stag-do) so I wasn’t going for a PB, but I still enjoyed the little extra _oomph_ you get when running with other people. parkrun isn’t a race, but it still encourages you to train and push yourself.

Over the last few years I’ve been in a really good habit with running, and I haven’t needed the motivation of a race in the diary (something others I’ve spoken to say they _do_ need), but I’d really like to beat my parkrun PB (21:12) that I set last September. So I’m starting to understand the appeal.

Every week there are people doing their first parkrun – walking or running (or a combination of the two). [Sign up](tab:https://www.parkrun.org.uk/register/) and give it a go.
