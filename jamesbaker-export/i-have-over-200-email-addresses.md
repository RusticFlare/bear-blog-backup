Up until last year I had one email address. I’d set it up when I was a teenager and used it for *everything*. After fifteen years it had reached a point where most of the emails I received were spam – the dodgy kind that you can’t unsubscribe from (I don’t trust any of the links) or block (each email is from a different address).  
  
I decided to fix this by making a new primary email address, and start the using the “Hide My Email” service from iCloud+.  
  
The new primary email address gave my inbox a fresh start.  
  
“Hide My Email” means for most websites, I use a unique address that forwards any emails to my inbox. I label each address with the site I use it for, and use a password manager to link them to passwords.  
  
Now, if I start getting any spam, I can deactivate the offending email address. Problem solved!  
  
I also started using [Kill The Newsletter](https://kill-the-newsletter.com/) for any mailing lists I subscribed to. This converts the emails into an RSS feed.

Changing everything over from my old account took a few evenings, and it’s been absolutely worth it.
  
Now my inbox is like a “To Do List”. Once an email comes in, I move it to the appropriate folder (Receipts/Tickets/Bills/etc…), and once I’ve actioned it, I delete it.
