Every once in a while you press play on an album that you’ve been looking forward to, and it delivers!

Yesterday, I got two of those in one day. 

- [_Modern Life Is Lonely_ – **Holding Absence**](tab:https://album.link/s/12qyF8VYd8ceuwyHpGGgw2)
- [_BLOOM_ – **EYRE LLEW**](tab:https://album.link/s/1HoeQ6aKUbC7MFjKFTCffG)

## _Modern Life Is Lonely_

![Album art of Modern Life Is Lonely](https://bear-images.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/jamesbaker/img_0337.webp)

My favourite [Baddiecore](/i-like-baddiecore/) album so far this year? I think so. I listened on my morning run. A near perfect mix of heavy, melody and emotion. The switching styles and electronic elements kept me engaged. I also love it when the opening and closing tracks are both bangers.

It doesn’t hurt that the album art’s great. I’ve ordered the vinyl, just to put it on my wall.

## _BLOOM_

![Album art of BLOOM](https://bear-images.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/jamesbaker/img_0338.webp)


Then for something completely different. 

If I hadn’t been listening to **Kerrang! Radio** one night at uni when they played [_The Deep_](tab:https://song.link/s/21XYECtpNRwJNH7hD2lTBR), there’s no way I’d have ever heard of **EYRE LLEW**: an atmospheric alt-rock band with only 1460 monthly listeners on Spotify. 

As someone who usually listens to Heavy Metal, **EYRE LLEW** have become my favourite band for quieter, more chill moments; like reading, or even drifting off to sleep.

_BLOOM_ is just what I wanted. A slight evolution (the lyrics are more in focus), but it still gets me into that relaxed state of mind.
