A few years ago I decided I’d only watch TV shows that were _finished_. I just got sick of waiting year(s) between series.

I’m still catching up on some shows I was already midway through:

- <cite>The Boys</cite>
- <cite>Good Omens</cite>
- <cite>Severance</cite>

Some of the shows I’d started watching got cancelled:

- <cite>Westworld</cite>
- <cite>Halo</cite>

I haven’t watched the final season of either.

Then there’s <cite>Ted Lasso</cite>, that ended – then started again! Or shows like <cite>Game of Thrones</cite>, that have spinoffs.

With anthology shows, like <cite>Inside No. 9</cite> and <cite>Black Mirror</cite>, I can just watch an episode wherever I feel like it. Same for some documentary series where there isn’t a narrative e.g. <cite>Louis Theroux</cite>. Then there’s <cite>Rick and Morty</cite> and <cite>South Park</cite> are in some weird middle ground between anthologies and ongoing story, so I also don’t wait for them to finish.

For some shows, you can watch each series independently, this includes most reality game shows, like <cite>Race Across the World</cite> and <cite>The Traitors</cite>.

And finally, live TV – sports and news – that you just watch as-and-when. <cite>Match of the Day</cite> also falls into this category for me.

On my TV show tracking app, I’ve started twelve shows that have episodes out which I haven’t watched. In an ideal world that list would be one.
