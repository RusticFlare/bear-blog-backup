Today was my third time running the _York 10 Mile_. My previous years were 2022 (when I ran 1:25:46) and 2024 (1:23:57).

Today, I really wanted to break the 1:20:00 mark. It’s actually a really nice number to pace: 5 min/km or 8 min/mile. So 10 miles in 80 minutes (1:20:00)!

I set myself up with a playlist where my favourite song for a sprint finish ([<cite>Bottom Feeder</cite> by Parkway Drive](tab:https://song.link/s/6jQu1YuisnFu36Sof5wjym)) should end at ~1:19:00. Jess then chose the order of the songs for me, so that listening would be a bit more interesting. [<cite>CAN YOU FEEL IT</cite> by Polyphia](tab:https://song.link/s/1UsjO2e3GW7mYpdWXyJ7AN) came on at the perfect time – just as I reached the first hill.

Ultimately, I did way better than I’d hoped and ran it in 1:15:14! That meant I had to start skipping songs on my playlist to try and get <cite>Bottom Feeder</cite> playing for the _evil_ hill they make you run up before the final _glorious_ 400m downhill to the finish. But something glitched and my music kept pausing. I ended up running the final kilometre with no music.

I’m proud of myself for getting a new PB and for smashing my goal. But now I’m looking at those 14 seconds! I guess I’ve got a new goal for next time.
